## Objectives and tracks

For n=3k+2, T=3k²+7k, preserve 3(f-T)=n+10-K, K=r+b+c.
Primary objective: K>=n-O(1). Competing objective: a genuinely renewable
construction with unbounded excess. Secondary objectives: T-O(1) lower
constructions and formalization of mature consequential results. The
reviewed unconditional upper remains T+floor(k/2)+1 until global progress
is proved; finite local improvements do not change it.

1. **Metric payment:** pay the actual height-weighted wide-bank residual
   from the retained account, or prove a maximum-specific normalization.
2. **Boundary coupling:** a tool for payment. Retain physical ports, actual
   component partitions, carrier connectivity, full new-edge bundles and
   cut costs. A neutral move produces no K credit.
3. **Construction/falsification:** k->k+2 has target increment 12k+26.
   A larger renewable gain needs actual connectivity and bounded cap loss.
4. **Verification:** independent proof review and computational audits;
   formalize stable results when they advance an active dependency.

## Progress and termination

Every active hypothesis specifies the global obligation it could remove,
where low K or maximum status enters, and its resource ownership. A new
subclass needs an explicit removed case and uncovered complement. Park
branches after a fatal counterexample or two cycles repeating a known
obstruction. Reject mere rearrangements of unchanged deficits.

## Constraints

* No optimization above side/dimension15; existing larger witnesses may
  be replayed/analyzed only. Two compute workers, 32 GB laptop.
* No competing full Lean builds. Preserve strict Lean requirements and
  no imposed timeout on the full formal verification command.
* Preserve user/staged work, historical source bytes and frozen artifacts.
  Do not stage, commit, amend or push without explicit authorization.
* Python packages: analysis/<subject>/, run as modules, mirrored tests
  when needed. Prefer domain-specific tools over orchestration machinery.
* Inspect repository files only. Do not inspect installed package sources,
  package caches or registry directories; this is an explicit user constraint.
* Distinguish grid realizations from abstract partition feasibility.
* Evidence labels: formal theorem; reviewed paper proof; unreviewed proof;
  complete finite result; heuristic evidence; open obligation.

## Records and reporting

`RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md` tracks statements and outcomes;
`RESEARCH_RESOURCES.md` tracks single ownership;
`research_batches/BATCH_001.md` begins the batch reports. Immutable data
records scope, complete cases/witnesses and audits. Reports answer what
was proved/refuted, which obligation shrank, review findings, stopped
branches, next three cycles, and decisions requiring the user. This
charter does not claim background execution between sessions.
