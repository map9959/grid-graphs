# Residue-Two Terminal Obstruction and B-State Test

This note records what the exact `n=17` frontier run says locally, gives the
strongest common finite control descriptor currently justified for the 179-U
and 250-U witnesses, and tests that descriptor at `n=23`.

## Exact terminal obstruction at 178 U

The global mirror-quotiented frontier computation is UNSAT at 178 U. The last
nonempty layer gives a more specific explanation. After cell `(16,14)`, at
step 287 of 289, exactly 20 states survive. Every survivor has:

```text
cost = 177
active dual vertices = OUT, face(15,14), face(15,15)
partition = [OUT] | [face(15,14)] | [face(15,15)]
```

Choosing dot at `(16,15)` retires `face(15,14)` in all 20 states. Choosing U
also retires that face in two states. In the other 18 states, choosing U joins
the two interior faces, but leaves two components and rank deficit one at the
full cost of 178. Thus the last available U can perform only one useful merge;
two merges are still needed.

The accepted 179-U affine witness reaches the same layer in a different state:

```text
cost = 178
partition = [OUT, face(15,14)] | [face(15,15)]
```

Its extra U has already attached `face(15,14)` to `OUT`. The U at `(16,15)`
then adds the edge between faces `(15,14)` and `(15,15)`, completing the second
merge at cost 179. The exact one-unit correction is therefore not just a count
deficit: it is one missing prior attachment. The machine-readable states and
replay are in `frontier_n17_u178_step287.json` and
`frontier_n17_u179_replay.json`.

An unmirrored repetition subsequently confirmed global UNSAT, independently
emptying at step 288 after 30,053,510,406 transitions. Its complete provenance
and final statistics are in `frontier_n17_unmirrored_result.json`.

## Candidate B control state

The 179-U affine witness at `n=17` and the 250-U phase-front witness at `n=20`
share this finite descriptor:

```text
surplus over the old q(n):        1
dual connectivity:                connected
dual cycle excess:                1 (unicyclic)
interior row form:                 . L^a C R^(k-a-|C|) .
center alphabet:                   X, H
maximum center width:              5
minimum strict moving-seam radius: 3
```

Here `L`, `R`, `X`, and `H` are the three-column packet symbols used by the
phase-front transducer. The endpoint data are concrete:

```text
state    U     dual V/E    cycle excess    maximum center width
A17     179     257/257          1                  4
B20     250     362/362          1                  5
```

Both endpoints independently pass dual-connectivity and forest-completion
validation. Their count increment is

```text
250 - 179 = 71 = q_old(20) - q_old(17),
```

so surplus one is preserved. A strict radius-3 moving-seam alignment connects
them with three HOLD rows and no boundary rewrites. The exact row words,
alignment certificate, seam schedule, rewrite census, and witness hash are in
`residue_two_b_state_analysis.json`, generated by
`analyze_residue_two_b_state.py`.

This is a concrete bounded carrier class, but it is not yet a proved recurrent
frontier state. In particular, equal surplus, cycle excess, and packet alphabet
do not prove that the two patterns have the same hidden connectivity signature
at every cut. The pump test below checks whether this candidate descriptor is
already strong enough.

## B20 to B23 pump test

The desired continuation is

```text
250 -> 333 U, an increment of 83 = q_old(23) - q_old(20).
```

Strict moving-seam alignment from B20 to the affine `n=23` control first exists
at radius 3; radii 0, 1, and 2 are incompatible. Complete radius-3 phase
enumeration is too large in Python. The exact calibrated subsearch therefore
uses:

* the lexicographically first strict radius-3 seam/HOLD schedule;
* affine rows `0..5` and `17..22` as fixed caps;
* every basic phase-carrier row over `X/H` with center width at most five in the
  mutable band;
* exact bidirectional frontier states joined after row 10;
* exact U cost, rank, and final connectivity checks.

The affine 335-U witness belongs to every row domain, so it is a positive
control for the entire pipeline. The completed run produced:

```text
budget   result               tested pairs   connected pairs   best profile
335      SAT                       34,096              705       [1,4]
333      UNSAT_IN_LANGUAGE             136                0       [3,3]
```

The reconstructed 335-U witness independently validates with all 485 dual
vertices connected and forest completion valid. At 333 U no compatible joined
state is connected. Full statistics and the positive witness are in
`residue_two_b20_to_b23_basic_result.json`; the run took 496.157 seconds.

The reconstructed basic near misses make the obstruction more concrete. The
best representative has an acyclic 154-face `OUT` component, a 330-face
component carrying all three cycles, and one isolated face. One-cell bridge
options exist at both missing cuts near rows 10 and 11, but no equal-count
one-for-one or two-for-two exchange through those bridge cells preserves dual
connectivity. The complete scoped exchange census is reproducible from
`analyze_residue_two_b23_near_miss_exchanges.py` and recorded in
`residue_two_b23_near_miss_exchanges.json`.

A targeted exact extension then allowed one `S=.U.` packet per row only at the
packet sites exposed by all three best near misses. It expanded the cut layers
to 187,997 top and 609,308 bottom states, retained the 335-U positive control,
and still produced exactly the same 136 cost-compatible 333-U pairs and the
same three best near misses. Its complete record is
`residue_two_b20_to_b23_targeted_result.json`.

The complete `X/H/S` phase carrier was subsequently ported to the compiled
row-frontier solver `src/bin/search_b23_phase_frontier.rs`. Calibration on the
basic domains reproduces every Python layer and join count while reducing wall
time from 502 seconds to 4.927 seconds. The full fixed-schedule phase run then
completed in 683.463 seconds:

```text
cut states: 1,677,381 top; 7,280,367 bottom
335 U:      137,366 tested pairs; 705 connected
333 U:          238 tested pairs;   0 connected; best 3 components
```

Thus the complete fixed-schedule `X/H/S` carrier does **not** pump from 250 U
to 333 U. The exact run is recorded in
`residue_two_b20_to_b23_phase_compiled_result.json`. This remains scoped: it is
not global `n=23` impossibility and does not exclude another strict radius-3
schedule, different caps, or a different surplus-one control state. The next
search should vary the schedule or the cap/control state rather than enlarge
this row alphabet again.
