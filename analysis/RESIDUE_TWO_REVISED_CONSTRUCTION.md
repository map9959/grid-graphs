# Revised Residue-Two Construction

## Result

No attachment-preserving recurrence with the revised target increment is
present in the existing patterns or controls.

For `n=3k+2`, `k>=5`, the revised target U count is

```text
T(k) = 6k^2 + 5k + 4,
T(k+1)-T(k) = 12k+11.
```

Since the enlarged board has `18k+21` new vertices, this is equivalently an
increase of `6k+10` full vertices. The arithmetic recurrence is realizable by
packet words, but the required rooted dual attachment recurrence is not.

The strongest verified recurrence remains the affine pump

```text
A(k) = 6k^2 + 6k - 1,
A(k+1)-A(k) = 12k+12.
```

It adds one too many U vertices, or one too few full vertices, at every step.
It is generated by duplicating rows 9--11 before row 9 and columns 4--6 before
column 4. Direct checks in `residue_two_revised_recurrence.py` verify every
member for `5<=k<=40` (`17<=n<=122`) is dual-connected and
forest-completable. This is finite verification, not an arbitrary-`k` proof.

## Exact-Charge Symbolic Attempt

The five cap rows used by A17 and B20 cost `8k+6`. The remaining `3k-3` rows
are words of `k` three-cell packets. Relative to two U cells per packet, use

```text
charge(H) = +1, charge(S) = -1,
charge(L) = charge(X) = charge(R) = 0.
```

Then the target is exactly equivalent to total mutable charge `3k-2`. A step
can therefore append one zero-charge packet to every old mutable row and insert
three new charge-one rows:

```text
cap change                 8
old mutable-row widening   2(3k-3)
three new rows             3(2(k+1)+1)
                           ----------
total                      12k+11.
```

`target_charge_step` implements this production symbolically. It appends an R
packet to every old mutable row, inserts three `H R^k` rows after five mutable
rows, and uses the corresponding five affine cap rows. Starting from the
connected 250-U B20 control, it has the exact target count for every generated
`k>=6`; the test checks the formula through `k=30`.

The first produced member, at `k=7`, has 333 U vertices as required but is not
a construction:

```text
n                         23
dual vertices             485
dual edges                483
dual components             8
OUT component vertices     64
component sizes      64,187,229,1,1,1,1,1
total cycle excess           6
forest completion        false (forced edges contain a cycle)
```

Thus packet charge alone does not preserve either dual connectivity or the
forced-forest condition. This uniform-right production is only a concrete
negative control, not a proof that all exact-charge productions fail.

## Missing Attachment Condition

The missing condition is equality of a **rooted open-slab interface**, not
endpoint connectivity, cycle excess, or membership in the same packet
alphabet. After deleting the new three-column slab and normalizing packet
coordinates, a valid recurrence must provide a cut where all of the following
are preserved:

1. The exposed U mask on the old ports.
2. The partition of all exposed dual faces, with the OUT block distinguished.
3. The complete attachment relation for components supported partly or wholly
   inside the deleted slab; no hidden component may be forgotten or retired.
4. The normalized U cost and dual rank/cycle payload needed by the continuation.
5. Enough new-slab edges to attach every new face while reproducing the same
   rooted interface at the opposite side.

The fifth item is the operative one-cell deficit: the production must spend
`12k+11`, attach all new faces, and return the source interface. Existing
controls can do the attachment at cost `12k+12`, or hit the target count while
leaving multiple rooted components.

This condition is forced by the committed diagnostics:

- A17 and B20 are both connected, unicyclic, target-count controls, but their
  selected alignment is not a state transfer. Across 58 comparable cuts only
  the first row of `8->11` preserves mask, partition, normalized cost, and cycle
  excess simultaneously. For `17->20`, compressed-mask equality occurs once,
  partition equality twice, normalized-cost equality never, and hidden-slab
  components occur at 18 of 19 cuts.
- The affine positive control has 40 composable two-sided landmark lanes, while
  A17 to B20 and B20 to affine A23 have none. This separates geometric packet
  similarity from attachment preservation.
- Exact target-recurrence searches for the two named HOLD schedules, both
  diagonal phases, all 65 charge-one HOLD rows, and independent radius-one
  ADVANCE seams have no connected 333-U join. The direct A17-to-n23 width-six
  compositions are also negative.
- Enlarging the two exposed attachment rows to all binary packets in a
  radius-two window, including mixed bottom-cap phases, remains exactly
  negative; every family has best profile `[2,1]`.
- The broader B20 self-renewal manifest does reproduce the B20 cap interfaces,
  but its exact product is computationally intractable. Beams through 20,000
  states per side find no connected 333-U pair and repeatedly stop at three
  components, while recovering connected 335-U controls. This is heuristic for
  333 U and must not be read as UNSAT.

## Verified Recurrence

The affine recurrence is currently the strongest positive result. From a
square pattern of width `n`, it uses

```text
rows:    0..8, 9,10,11, 9..n-1
columns: 0..3, 4,5,6, 4..n-1.
```

`affine_candidate(k)` reconstructs this recurrence independently from A17 and
agrees exactly with `diagonal_patterns.residue_two_affine_pattern`. For each
tested step it directly checks

```text
width increase             3
U increase                 12k+12
full-vertex increase       6k+9
dual reached/total         equal
forest completion          valid
```

The recurrence has surplus `k-5` over the revised target. Therefore it proves a
uniform lower construction with one additional U per pump, but it does not
settle the requested revised bound.
